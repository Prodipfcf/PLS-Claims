Why PLS

Reasons to use our service

Senior-level claim specialists

All of our claim specialists are senior-level experts in their field and have the experience to give cases the due diligence and specialized attention they need.

Early resolution

We proactively work to resolve claims early, before they escalate to litigation, by identifying key defense-strategy elements in the early stages of a claim wherever possible.

Litigated claims

We have extensive experience managing litigated claims, including managing budgets and activity and collaborating with defense counsel to manage mediation and litigation strategy.

Cost containment

Costs and fees are carefully managed on every claim. We review, manage, alter and approve defense counsel and other fees throughout the life of a claim, which allows us to be as proactive as desired in managing the cost of administering each claim.

Reporting requirements

We handle all state and federal reporting requirements and releases where applicable.
