About PLS

The right expertise to manage your claims

Experienced claims professionals focused on diligent, proactive claims administration.

We are highly experienced claims management professionals who work diligently and proactively to administer claims in a timely manner, from initial report through to resolution and final disposition.

PLS's claims management work includes litigation budgets, defense strategy, defense attorney activity and spending, reserve-setting and management, medical records review, mediation negotiations and settlements, reporting requirements, loss runs, and expense management.

Why PLS

Reasons to use our service

Senior-level claims specialists

All of our claims specialists are senior-level experts in their field and have the experience to give cases due diligence and specialized attention.

Early resolution

We proactively work to resolve claims early, before they escalate to litigation, by identifying key defense-strategy elements in the early stages of a claim wherever possible.

Litigated claims

We have extensive experience managing litigated claims, including managing budgets and activity and collaborating with defense counsel on mediation and litigation strategy.

Cost containment

Costs and fees are carefully managed on every claim. We review, manage, alter and approve defense counsel and other fees throughout the life of a claim.

Reporting requirements

We handle all state and federal reporting requirements and releases where applicable.
