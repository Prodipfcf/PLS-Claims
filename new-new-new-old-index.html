About PLS

The right expertise to manage your claims

PLS provides full-spectrum insurance claims management and administration. Its claims professionals work proactively from initial report through resolution and final disposition, with support across litigation budgets, defense strategy, reserve management, reporting, mediation and expense management.

Areas of claims support

  • Litigation budgets
  • Defense strategy
  • Defense attorney activity and spending
  • Reserve-setting and management
  • Peer review panel hearing preparation
  • Medical records review
  • Expert retention
  • Expert witness summaries
  • Mediation negotiations and settlements
  • Reporting requirements and payments for professional liability claims
  • Loss runs
  • Expense management

Why PLS

A focused approach to claims management

The following points reflect the claims management approach described in the existing PLS content.

Senior-level claims specialists

Our claims specialists are senior-level experts in their field, with the experience to give each case due diligence and specialized attention.

Early resolution

We work proactively to resolve claims early, before they escalate to litigation, by identifying key defense-strategy elements in the early stages wherever possible.

Litigated claims

We have extensive experience managing litigated claims, including budgets and activity, and collaborating with defense counsel on mediation and litigation strategy.

Cost containment

Costs and fees are carefully managed on each claim. We review and manage defense counsel and other fees throughout the life of a claim.

Reporting requirements

We handle applicable state and federal reporting requirements and releases.
